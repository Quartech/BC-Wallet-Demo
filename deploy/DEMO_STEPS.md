# Demo Steps - BC Wallet Showcases

This document provides step-by-step instructions for demonstrating the two BC Wallet showcases that demonstrate digital credential issuance and verification workflows.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the demos, ensure you have:
- A smartphone with the BC Wallet app installed
- Access to the showcase URLs
- A QR code scanner (built into most smartphones)

---

## Showcase 1: California DMV mDL Showcase

**URL:** https://showcase-manager-bc-wallet-demo-web.trustservices.quartech.com/digital-trust/showcase/showcase-manager-tenant/california-dmv-mdl-showcase

**Purpose:** This showcase demonstrates how mobile driver's licenses (mDL) can be issued and used for identity verification. In the L.A. Games scenario, this mDL credential serves as a prerequisite for obtaining volunteer credentials.

### Demo Flow Overview
1. **Install BC Wallet** - Set up the digital wallet application
2. **Connect with California DMV** - Establish secure connection with the issuing authority
3. **Accept mDL Credential** - Receive and store the digital driver's license
4. **Share Credential** - Demonstrate how to share the credential for verification
5. **Complete Verification** - Show the verification process with minimal data sharing

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Install BC Wallet
- **Reading Instructions:** "First, install the BC Wallet app onto your smartphone. Select the button below for instructions and the next step."
- **Demo Action:** Guide users to install the BC Wallet app from their device's app store
- **Key Point:** Emphasize that this is the foundation for all digital credential interactions

#### Step 2: Connect with California DMV
- **Reading Instructions:** "Imagine, as John Doe, you are logged into the California DMV website. They want to offer you a Mobile Driver's License. Use your BC Wallet to scan the QR code from the website."
- **Demo Action:** Scan the QR code displayed on the showcase page
- **Key Point:** Demonstrate the secure connection establishment process

#### Step 3: Accept your mobile driver's license
- **Reading Instructions:** "Your wallet now has a secure and private connection with California DMV. You should have received an offer in BC Wallet for a Mobile Driver's License. Review what they are sending, and choose 'Accept offer'."
- **Demo Action:** Show the credential offer in BC Wallet and accept it
- **Key Point:** Highlight the transparency of what data is being requested

#### Step 4: You're all set!
- **Reading Instructions:** "Congratulations, you've just received your mobile driver's license credential. It is safely stored in your wallet and ready to be used for identity verification. This credential can now be used to verify your identity for other services, including volunteer applications for the L.A. Games."
- **Demo Action:** Confirm the credential is stored in the wallet
- **Key Point:** Emphasize the credential's reusability across different services

#### Step 5: Scan the QR code to start sharing
- **Reading Instructions:** "Open the BC Wallet app and scan the QR code on the L.A. Games website to start sharing your mobile driver's license credential."
- **Demo Action:** Scan the verification QR code
- **Key Point:** Show how credentials can be shared for verification

#### Step 6: Confirm the information to send
- **Reading Instructions:** "BC Wallet will now ask you to confirm what to send. Notice how it will only share if the credential has not expired, not even the expiry date itself gets shared. You don't have to share anything else for it to be trustable."
- **Demo Action:** Review and confirm the sharing permissions
- **Key Point:** Emphasize privacy and minimal data sharing principles

#### Step 7: You are done!
- **Reading Instructions:** "You proved your identity using your mobile driver's license, and the L.A. Games can now verify who you are. It only took a few seconds, you revealed minimal information, and the L.A. Games could easily and automatically trust what you sent. This credential is now ready to be used for other services that require identity verification."
- **Demo Action:** Complete the verification process
- **Key Point:** Highlight the efficiency, privacy, and trust aspects of the system

## Demo Tips and Best Practices

### Before Starting
- Ensure BC Wallet is properly installed and set up
- Test QR code scanning functionality
- Have both showcase URLs ready to access

### During the Demo
- **Pace:** Take time to explain each step and its significance
- **Privacy Focus:** Emphasize the privacy-preserving aspects of the system
- **Efficiency:** Highlight the speed and convenience of digital credentials
- **Trust:** Explain how cryptographic verification builds trust
- **User Control:** Demonstrate how users maintain control over their data

### Key Talking Points
1. **Privacy by Design:** Users only share what's necessary and maintain control
2. **Interoperability:** Credentials work across different services and organizations
3. **Security:** Cryptographic verification ensures authenticity and integrity
4. **User Experience:** Simple, fast, and convenient credential management
5. **Trust Framework:** Organizations can trust credentials without storing personal data

### Common Questions to Address
- How is this different from traditional ID systems?
- What happens if I lose my phone?
- How do organizations verify the credentials?
- Is my personal data stored by the organizations?
- Can credentials be revoked or updated?
